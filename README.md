TrustLayer

Reputation belongs to people, not platforms.

TrustLayer is a decentralized reputation infrastructure designed for peer-to-peer trading.

Unlike traditional marketplaces, TrustLayer does not list products, process payments, or replace existing trading platforms.

Instead, it enables people to build, own, and carry their trading reputation across different marketplaces.

⸻

Why TrustLayer?

Today, trust is fragmented.

A person may spend years building an excellent reputation on Facebook Marketplace, TradeMe, or other online communities.

However, that reputation belongs to the platform.

If the user changes platform, loses an account, or the platform changes its policies, years of reputation may disappear.

TrustLayer believes that reputation should belong to the person who earned it.

⸻

Our Mission

Reputation belongs to people, not platforms.

People create reputation through honest behaviour.

Blockchain preserves reputation by protecting immutable historical records.

Marketplaces simply reference reputation—they do not own it.

⸻

What TrustLayer Is

TrustLayer is:

* A decentralized reputation infrastructure
* A portable trading reputation system
* A tamper-resistant reputation ledger
* An open reputation API for marketplaces
* A long-term digital reputation protocol

⸻

What TrustLayer Is NOT

TrustLayer is NOT:

* A marketplace
* A payment platform
* An escrow service
* A social network
* A universal credit scoring system

Existing marketplaces continue to provide listings, messaging, logistics, and payments.

TrustLayer only provides reputation services.

⸻

Design Principles

Our design follows several fundamental principles.

1. Reputation belongs to people.
2. Reputation is earned through real behaviour.
3. Blockchain records history rather than judging people.
4. Reputation history is append-only.
5. Marketplaces contribute reputation events instead of owning reputation.
6. Technology serves philosophy, not the other way around.

⸻

MVP (Phase 1)

The first version focuses only on Trading Reputation.

Features include:

* User identity
* Reputation profile
* Trade event recording
* Reputation history
* Reputation viewer

No payment escrow.

No dispute arbitration.

No marketplace functionality.

⸻

Long-term Vision

TrustLayer begins with peer-to-peer trading.

In the future, the same reputation infrastructure may support:

* Rental reputation
* Freelancing reputation
* Professional reputation
* Community contribution
* Open-source contribution

Each reputation domain remains independent while sharing the same decentralized foundation.
